<h1>The 2024 30 Day Chart Challenge</h1>
This challenge asks data scientists, data analysts, and science communicators to create a new chart every day during the month of April.  Each chart is based on a prompt or particular data source, but otherwise participants have free reign in what they choose to explore, how to present their data, and where they display it.
